### Insurance Risk (2)

- **When Actuaries See The Future: Climate Risk, Insurance, And Your Wallet**
  - **Categories:** Climate Risk, Insurance Risk
  - Bill Frist explains how actuaries translate uncertain climate hazards into premiums, reserves and risk-pooling decisions. The article emphasizes cumulative flood risk, correlated and cascading catastrophe losses, the role of climate projections and reinsurance, and the way changes in insurance availability and cost affect households and wider financial markets.
  - **Keywords:** climate risk, actuaries, insurance, personal finance
  🔗 https://www.forbes.com/sites/billfrist/2026/01/21/when-actuaries-see-the-future-climate-risk-insurance-and-your-wallet/

- **How is insurance underwriting impacted by climate change? - LSE**
  - **Categories:** Insurance Risk, Actuarial Modelling
  - LSE explains that changing climate hazards make underwriting losses more severe and less predictable, prompting changes in premiums, terms and coverage availability. Climate scenarios, catastrophe models and attribution science can improve assessment, but longer-term insights do not automatically enter short-term contracts. The article also stresses loss-prevention measures and the consequences of insurance retreat.
  - **Keywords:** insurance underwriting, climate change, loss forecasting, risk models
  🔗 https://www.lse.ac.uk/granthaminstitute/explainers/how-is-insurance-underwriting-impacted-by-climate-change/

Its 69 indicators support screening and the prioritization of adaptation resources, while highlighting that high climate-related health risk often coincides with limited access to essential health services.
  - **Keywords:** climate health, risk index, public health, resource allocation

### Insurance Risk (1)

- **Developing Parametric Insurance for Weather Related Risks**
  - **Categories:** Insurance Risk, Adaptation & Resilience
  - The World Bank's technical note examines parametric weather-risk insurance for Indonesia within a broader disaster-financing strategy. It describes transferring predefined event risks to reinsurance or capital markets, alongside reserves and contingent finance. Index-based payouts can support post-disaster liquidity, but basis risk means payments may differ from actual losses.
  - **Keywords:** parametric insurance, weather risk, climate adaptation, catastrophe bonds
