### Verified information

Nearly two years after its launch in 2024, the US$ 3.6 million, four-year Climate Risk and Early Warning Systems (CREWS) Djibouti initiative is already delivering tangible results. Jointly implemented by the World Meteorological Organization (WMO) and the United Nations Office for Disaster Risk Reduction (UNDRR), the project is strengthening Djibouti's ability to anticipate and respond to flash floods, landslides and other hydrometeorological hazards.
