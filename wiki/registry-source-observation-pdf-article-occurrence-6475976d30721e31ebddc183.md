WMO bulletin shows successes and challenges in ozone layer recovery

The annual ozone bulletin reports the 2025 Antarctic ozone hole as the fourth-to-fifth smallest since severe
depletion was first observed in 1992, with full recovery expected within several decades, and records UV
radiation rising by as much as 20% in parts of Europe since the mid-1990s, driven mainly by reduced cloud
cover. Marginal but retained on two counts: the Kigali Amendment HFC phase-down is a live transition-risk
exposure for the cooling and refrigeration value chain, and the UV trend is a slow-burn morbidity signal for life
and health portfolios.
