## Registry article-version summary

In the wake of recent severe weather events — including the US bomb cyclone and UK Storms Ingrid and Chandra — a critical gap in business interruption (BI) insurance coverage for small and medium enterprises (SMEs) has come into focus. Many SMEs that suffered days of forced closure found their claims denied or delayed, and with natural disaster losses reaching ~$131 billion in H1 2025 (only ~$80 billion insured), the uninsured gap exceeds Bahrain's GDP. Current BI policy terms are inadequate for modern climate volatility — a direct call for product innovation in the actuarial and underwriting community.
