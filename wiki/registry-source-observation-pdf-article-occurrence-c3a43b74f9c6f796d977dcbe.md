Summer storms across the Canadian Prairies caused over $1.8bn in insured losses: CatIQ
IN WINDOW 24 SEP 2026 DATA CATASTROPHE RISK
CatIQ tallies over CAD 1.8 billion of insured losses from ten extreme weather events across Alberta,
Saskatchewan and Manitoba in summer 2026, the 9–10 June Saskatchewan/Manitoba severe storms alone at
CAD 900 million. Notably, flooding displaced the usual hail and wind as the dominant Prairie peril, with record
June and July rainfall in Edmonton. Insurance Bureau of Canada context: insured extreme-weather losses are
up roughly 320% since the late 1990s, with 2024 a record CAD 9.4 billion. A peril-mix shift of this kind is a direct
challenge to the hazard weights in Canadian property models.
