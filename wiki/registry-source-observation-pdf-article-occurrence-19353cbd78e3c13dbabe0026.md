STATEMENT: US EPA Repeals Climate Pollution Rules for Power Plants
IN WINDOW 14 SEP 2026 PRESS RELEASE TRANSITION RISK
EPA finalised a rule eliminating carbon emission limits on fossil-fuel-fired power plants, the sector responsible for
roughly a quarter of US climate pollution and its second-largest emissions source. WRI puts fossil electricity's
health toll at tens of thousands of avoidable impacts and hundreds of billions of dollars in health costs annually,
separate from climate-disaster costs averaging USD 150 billion a year. Following EPA's February 2026 reversal
of its own authority to limit climate pollution, this is a material transition-risk and litigation-risk data point for US
utility and energy exposures, and a concrete argument for reweighting away from rapid-transition scenarios in
US-centric books.
