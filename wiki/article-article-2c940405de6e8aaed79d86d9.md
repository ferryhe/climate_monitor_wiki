## Registry article-version summary

Actuarial analysis noting that California wildfire has evolved from a secondary concern to a primary catastrophe peril. The $40B+ insured losses from January 2025 LA fires combined with $50B severe convective storm losses in 2025 demonstrate catastrophe risk extends well beyond hurricanes and earthquakes. Actuaries building catastrophe models focused primarily on hurricane and earthquake perils may be underweighting wildfire's growing contribution.
