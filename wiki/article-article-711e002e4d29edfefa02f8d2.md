GENEVA, Aug 11 – More than 120 IPCC authors are meeting in Mexico City, Mexico, from 11 to 14 August to advance the First Order Draft of the 2027 Methodology Report on Carbon Dioxide Removal Technologies, Carbon Capture, Utilization, and Storage (CDR-CCUS).
