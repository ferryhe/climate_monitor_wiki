Why circularity needs to be at the heart of industrial decarbonization
IN WINDOW 14 SEP 2026 BLOG TRANSITION RISK
Resource extraction and processing accounted for more than 55% of global emissions in 2022 and could rise
almost 60% from 2020 levels by 2060 absent intervention, while only 6.9% of raw materials currently cycle back
into the economy after first use; over 90% of third-generation NDCs name industry as a priority mitigation sector.
Material-efficiency pathways change the abatement cost curve, and therefore the plausibility of the industrial
decarbonisation assumptions embedded in climate scenario analysis for heavy-industry credit and equity
exposures.
