Climate Change El Niño’s hidden losses: when one season’s damage fuels the next crisis 20/07/2026 When El Niño-linked weather extremes hit agrifood systems, the damage can be counted in failed crops, dead livestock, flooded fields and lost income. But the real cost often begins where that first tally ends. One failed season can degrade soils, deplete seed stocks, reduce herds and deepen debt, leaving families less able to recover before the next climate crisis hits.
