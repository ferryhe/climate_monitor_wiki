## Registry article-version summary

Overview of parametric insurance as a protection-gap tool: rapid trigger-based payouts for rainfall, wind and heat events. Flags basis risk and product sustainability as payout frequency rises — a core pricing and trigger-design concern for actuaries.

## Report observation: climate-monitor-2026-08-10.md

Parametric policies pay when measurable weather conditions cross agreed thresholds, enabling faster and more flexible payments while covering some climate risks that conventional indemnity insurance may leave unprotected. Satellite and AI-enabled products can expand coverage, but threshold-dependent design can leave losses unpaid and increasingly frequent climate events may challenge product sustainability.
