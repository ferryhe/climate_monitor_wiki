## Article semantic summary

Swiss Re Institute and Insurance Journal report that wildfires, severe convective storms, and floods accounted for a record 92% of global insured natural catastrophe losses in 2025 (totalling US$107bn), with wildfires alone causing a record US$40bn from Los Angeles fires. 2026 forecast: US$148bn baseline, US$320bn peak-loss scenario. Secondary perils (wildfires, SCS, floods) drove 92% of 2025 losses.
