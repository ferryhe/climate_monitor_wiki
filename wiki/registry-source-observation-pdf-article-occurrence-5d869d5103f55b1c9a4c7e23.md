How climate action can reduce security risks in the Hindu Kush Himalaya
IN WINDOW 16 SEP 2026 BLOG PHYSICAL RISK
The Hindu Kush Himalaya supplies water to over 240 million people in the region and nearly two billion
downstream across 16 countries. Glacial ice was lost 65% faster in the 2010s than in the previous decade, with
projected 80% volume loss by 2100 on current emissions; the 2022 South Asian heatwave affected over one
billion people and the 2022 Pakistan floods hit 33 million people with over USD 30 billion in damage. Relevant to
compound physical-risk and correlated-loss assessment — glacial lake outburst flood, riverine flood and heat —
across a transboundary exposure base with very thin insurance penetration.
