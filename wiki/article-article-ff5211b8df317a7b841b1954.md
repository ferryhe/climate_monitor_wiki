## Article semantic summary

Environmental and Social Sustainability Framework (ESSF) helps ensure that UNEP programmes and projects protect people and the environment, uphold human rights and deliver lasting benefits. It integrates environmental, social and economic considerations throughout their design and implementation, sets clear sustainability standards and strengthens UNEP’s accountability to affected communities, our Member States and funders. It also promotes meaningful stakeholder participation, helps identify and reduce potential risks and harm, and provides channels for raising concerns.
