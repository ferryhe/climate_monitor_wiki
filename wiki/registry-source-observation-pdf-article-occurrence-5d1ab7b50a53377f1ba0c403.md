Swiss Re highlights growing catastrophe protection gap as nat cat losses rise 5-7% annually
IN WINDOW 14 SEP 2026 RESEARCH CATASTROPHE RISK
Swiss Re puts structural trend growth in insured nat-cat losses at 5–7% a year, with European wildfire losses
compounding an estimated 8–11% annually over recent decades. Modelled insured losses could reach roughly
USD 320 billion in a 2026 peak-loss scenario, and a 2017-style hurricane cluster alone pushes annual insured
losses above USD 120 billion, against 2025 global insured nat-cat losses of USD 120 billion. The 5–7% trend
rate is the single most citable figure this fortnight for calibrating loss-trend assumptions in catastrophe pricing
and ORSA scenarios.
