## Registry article-version summary

A new MSCI Institute report (April 8, 2026) finds that physical climate risks are increasingly viewed as a systemic financial stability issue, not just an insurance sector concern. The report notes $107 billion in global insured nat cat losses in 2025, with secondary perils (floods, wildfires, severe storms) accounting for a growing share — challenging the traditional reliance on historical loss data for pricing. It flags that insurance availability is becoming a critical enabler (or barrier) for infrastructure project financing, and highlights parametric insurance as a growing complementary risk management tool.
