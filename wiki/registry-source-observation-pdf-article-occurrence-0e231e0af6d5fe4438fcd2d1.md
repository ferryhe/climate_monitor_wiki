Property aggregate XoL rebounds with 50% increase in traded limits: Marsh Re
IN WINDOW 21 SEP 2026 DATA REINSURANCE
Marsh Re reports a 50% increase in traded limits for property aggregate excess-of-loss, with nearly 40% of
buyers now building frequency protection into excess-of-loss strategies, up from just over 25% two years ago;
property retro quota share limits placed exceed USD 20 billion, up 30% over four years. The return of aggregate
and frequency cover is the market's direct response to secondary-peril loss frequency — the structural feature
that had been priced out after 2022 — and it changes the shape of the protection available for climate-driven
attritional loss.
