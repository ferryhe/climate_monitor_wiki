Pacific ministers chart course for stronger weather and climate services
IN WINDOW 19 SEP 2026 PRESS RELEASE DISASTER RISK FINANCING
The Fourth Pacific Meteorological Ministers Meeting in Nuku'alofa adopted the Siu-í-Álaimoana-Ki-Likutapu
Declaration committing to stronger National Meteorological and Hydrological Services and expanded multi-
hazard early warning, and advanced a Pacific Islands Meteorological Strategy 2027–2036. Tonga's Prime
Minister put the region's average annual cost of weather and climate events at half a billion US dollars, and the
meeting flagged El Niño conditions expected to peak between November 2026 and January 2027. Directly
relevant to Pacific disaster-risk-financing and parametric programme design, and to the early-warning cost–
benefit case underpinning resilience-linked pricing.
