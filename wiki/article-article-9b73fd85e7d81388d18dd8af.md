## Registry article-version summary

The UNDP is joining governments, IFIs, and private investors at the 2026 FfD Forum in New York (20–24 April) as development assistance and humanitarian aid are in sharp decline while debt service costs in developing countries have reached 20-year highs. The forum will focus on closing the $4.3 trillion annual SDG financing gap by strengthening country financing systems, aligning public finance with private investment, and scaling blended finance instruments — directly relevant to climate adaptation and resilience financing.
