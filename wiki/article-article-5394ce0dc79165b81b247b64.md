## Article semantic summary

A new analysis from InsuranceERM finds that the growing talent shortage in the insurance and reinsurance sector has escalated into a strategic-level business risk, with firms struggling to recruit and retain staff with actuarial, risk management, and climate analytics expertise. As climate-related risks grow more complex, the skills gap is impairing insurers' ability to model, price, and manage these risks effectively. This finding has direct implications for actuarial function resourcing and the future pipeline of climate-capable professionals.
