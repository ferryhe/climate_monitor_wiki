Global Taxation of Agriculture and Forestry (WP/26/200)
IN WINDOW SEPTEMBER 2026 WORKING PAPER CARBON PRICING
Surveys domestic tax treatment of agriculture and forestry across 89 countries including environmental tax
measures, and notes agriculture's role as a contributor to greenhouse gas emissions. Land-use and agricultural
taxation is the missing leg of most carbon-pricing coverage analysis, so this bears on anyone modelling the
forward path of carbon prices or agricultural transition risk — though climate is one objective among several here
rather than the organising frame.
Caveat. Partial verification. Title, paper number and authorship were read first-hand from the PDF cover, but IMF PDF

inside 14–27 September rather than 5–13 September. Treat the date as September 2026.
