NZAOA calls on private market asset managers to turn climate ambition into everyday practice
IN WINDOW 17 SEP 2026 GUIDANCE TRANSITION RISK
The 85-member Net-Zero Asset Owner Alliance published an updated Call to Action to Private Market Asset
Managers setting seven expectations of general partners across private equity, private debt, infrastructure and
real estate, including firm-level net-zero alignment, better financed-emissions data quality, engagement-led
decarbonisation and explicit integration of physical climate risk analysis, anchored on a principle of acting on
data available today rather than waiting for perfect data. Material for life and pension actuaries: private markets
are the segment where climate data is weakest and valuations least mark-to-market, and asset owners are now
setting explicit physical-risk expectations on general partners.
