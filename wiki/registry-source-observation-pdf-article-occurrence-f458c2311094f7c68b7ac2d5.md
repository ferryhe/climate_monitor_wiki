### Verified information

When people think about climate vulnerability, they often picture devastating cyclones and coastlines disappearing beneath rising seas. Yet some of the world's biggest climate risks are unfolding hundreds of kilometers from the ocean. In many landlocked countries, climate change is causing severe changes to the glaciers, lakes and rivers that make life possible.
