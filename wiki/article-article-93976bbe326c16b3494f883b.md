## Article semantic summary

What Panasonic calls its “Contribution Impact” is widely known as “avoided emissions”. The World Business Council for Sustainable Development (WBCSD) defines avoided emissions as “positive impact created when comparing the GHG impact of a low-carbon solution to an alternative scenario without that solution in place.” For certain industries, the concept is nothing new. Back in 2013, the chemical industry published a methodology document to assess and report avoided emissions.

## Report observation: climate-monitor-2026-08-10.md

Avoided emissions compare the greenhouse-gas impact of a low-carbon solution with a scenario in which that solution is absent, capturing customer-side reductions not fully recognized by Scope 1–3 accounting. They should be reported separately from GHG inventories; broader use in investment and lending could reward low-carbon technologies, but methodologies still require clearer baselines, reference scenarios, attribution and calculation disclosure.
