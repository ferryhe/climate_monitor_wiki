Extreme heat is putting 2.4 billion workers at risk. Here's how 4 companies are responding
IN WINDOW 22 SEP 2026 RESEARCH CLIMATE AND HEALTH
WEF/BCG analysis: 2.4 billion workers, 70% of the global workforce, are exposed to excessive heat, with nearly
19,000 heat-linked worker deaths and 23 million injuries annually and projected productivity losses of USD 2.4
trillion a year by 2030. The implementation gap is the actuarially interesting number — 80% of companies name
extreme heat a business threat but only 28% have piloted interventions. Bayer reports a 20% reduction in heat-
related incidents in North America and USD 3,000 in avoided medical and lost-time costs per prevented claim, a
usable data point for workers' compensation and group health pricing.
