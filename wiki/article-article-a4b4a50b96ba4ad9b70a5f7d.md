The World Bank's Climate and Health Risk Index combines exposure, population sensitivity and adaptive capacity to compare relative health risks across countries and subnational areas. Its 69 indicators support screening and the prioritization of adaptation resources, while highlighting that high climate-related health risk often coincides with limited access to essential health services.
