STATEMENT: UN Secretary-General's Climate Summit Calls for Faster, Fairer Climate Action
IN WINDOW 23 SEP 2026 PRESS RELEASE ADAPTATION
WRI's response to the UNSG Climate Summit, organised around keeping 1.5°C within reach, mobilising finance
for developing countries, and — new emphasis — advancing adaptation during climate overshoot. Germany
announced a national fossil-fuel phase-out roadmap to 2045; WRI cites a benefit-cost ratio of more than 10:1
over a decade for adaptation spending. The formal arrival of “adaptation under overshoot” in the multilateral
agenda is the signal for scenario designers: it legitimises planning around temporary exceedance of 1.5°C rather
than treating it as a tail case.
