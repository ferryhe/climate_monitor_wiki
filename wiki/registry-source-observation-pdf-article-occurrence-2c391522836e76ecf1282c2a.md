World Trade Report: Trade reform to boost growth; inaction can cost 10% global GDP
IN WINDOW 15 SEP 2026 REPORT
The 2026 World Trade Report, launched at the opening of the Public Forum, finds a strengthened multilateral
trading system could increase global GDP by roughly 3% or USD 3 trillion by 2050, while failure to modernise
could reduce global output by up to 10%. Climate content is thin — the environmental transition is named as one
of the forces reshaping trade but the report is not structured around climate. Retained because the macro-
fragmentation assumptions feed directly into the narrative layer of transition scenarios, and because
fragmentation is the same mechanism that constrains cross-border reinsurance capacity.
