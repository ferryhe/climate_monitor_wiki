Addis Ababa Call to Action on Early Warnings in Africa
IN WINDOW 7 SEP 2026 INITIATIVE
Output of the Africa Early Warnings Forum held 2–4 September: 41 African countries now report on warning
systems and comprehensiveness has more than doubled since 2022, but gaps persist in small island states,
least-developed countries and fragile states. Sets five priorities including dedicated domestic budget lines and
linking warnings to anticipatory action — directly relevant to parametric and anticipatory-action product design
and to protection-gap analysis.

Maintain Early Warnings for All as a global political priority, working towards universal, effective and sustainable multi-hazard early warning coverage. The progress achieved in Africa before and since 2022 must be accelerated, sustained, and driven by the priorities and leadership of African countries and communities.
