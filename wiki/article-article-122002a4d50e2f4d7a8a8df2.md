## Article semantic summary

According to UNEP's 2025 Adaptation Gap Report, developing countries require an estimated USD 310–365 billion annually for climate adaptation by 2035, while current international public finance falls far short of this need. UNDP has developed the Adaptation Finance Strategy Guideline (AFSG) as a practical framework for governments to mobilize and align finance for adaptation investments identified in National Adaptation Plans (NAPs) and NDCs.
