## Article semantic summary

Organised in collaboration with the Met Office, the conference will examine how climate change is reshaping the boundary of insurability. Changing hazard patterns, growing concentrations of exposure, and rising repair and reinsurance costs are placing pressure on households, businesses, public authorities, insurers and reinsurers. Traditional indemnity insurance remains central, but parametric and hybrid structures may provide faster and more predictable liquidity, complement existing cover, and help address protection gaps.
