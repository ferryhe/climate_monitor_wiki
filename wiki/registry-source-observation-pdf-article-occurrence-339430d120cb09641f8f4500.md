Panel to review EU carbon border adjustment mechanism and emissions trading scheme
IN WINDOW 25 SEP 2026 DISPUTE TRANSITION RISK LITIGATION RISK
The Dispute Settlement Body agreed on 25 September to the Russian Federation's request for a panel to review
the EU's Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism package and an alleged export subsidy under the EU emissions
allowance trading scheme — the request having first been considered and blocked at the DSB meeting of 24
July 2026. This is the first time a carbon border measure will be adjudicated under WTO dispute settlement. For
actuaries the significance is twofold: it converts CBAM from a settled policy parameter into a contested one,
which widens the plausible range of carbon-price and border-adjustment assumptions in transition scenarios for
EU-exposed industrials; and it is the clearest instance yet of climate policy generating first-order international
liability risk. The panel's timetable is not yet published.
