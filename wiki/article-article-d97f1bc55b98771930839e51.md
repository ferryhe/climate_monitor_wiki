Despite Colorado State University's projection of a below-average 2026 Atlantic hurricane season (13 named storms, 6 hurricanes, 2 major hurricanes), catastrophe risk experts warn that a single landfalling storm remains capable of driving significant insured losses. Recent catastrophe data shows that loss levels remain structurally elevated even in below-average years, driven by increasing exposure concentration in coastal and wildfire-adjacent zones. Actuaries are advised to maintain full hurricane loadings in pricing and reserves regardless of seasonal outlooks.
