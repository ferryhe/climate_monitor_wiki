WMO: Recent water trends have serious future implications
IN WINDOW 17 SEP 2026 REPORT PHYSICAL RISK
Launch of the State of Global Water Resources 2025. 2025 was among the driest years for river discharge in
over 35 years, with below-normal flows across 36% of global basin area; for the seventh consecutive year basins
in normal condition were a minority at 34–38% against a 1991–2020 norm of 46%. Glaciers lost mass for a
fourth straight year across all regions, 1,400 gigatonnes over 2023–2025, and nearly two-thirds of monitored
groundwater wells sat outside historical norms. Asia and Africa accounted for at least 50% of water-related
extreme events and over 80% of associated deaths in the last five years. The headline finding — that the cycle
now oscillates between too little and too much rather than trending in one direction — directly degrades
stationarity assumptions in flood and drought models.
