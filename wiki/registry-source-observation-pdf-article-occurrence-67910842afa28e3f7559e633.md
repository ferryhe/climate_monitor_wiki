Mozambique advances validation of Climate Strategy to 2035
IN WINDOW 22 SEP 2026 PRESS RELEASE
A five-day technical workshop opening 14 September pre-validated Mozambique's updated National Strategy for
Climate Change Adaptation and Mitigation (ENAMMC) to 2035, covering cyclones, floods, droughts and coastal
erosion, with an Action Plan, Resource Mobilisation Strategy and Communication Mechanism, funded through
AfDB's Africa NDC Hub and the Africa Climate Change Fund. Mozambique is among the most cyclone-exposed
economies in the world; the Resource Mobilisation Strategy is the document to which any future sovereign risk
transfer would be anchored.
