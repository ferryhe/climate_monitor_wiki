## Registry article-version summary

The International Research Institute for Climate and Society (IRI) at Columbia University reports that the equatorial Pacific is transitioning from declining La Nina conditions toward ENSO-neutral, with a 90% probability for March-May 2026. However, El Nino probabilities rise rapidly from May onward, reaching 72-80% by July-September 2026. For insurance and actuarial users, the IRI forecast provides critical forward-looking climate intelligence: the shift toward El Nino conditions in mid-2026 would alter tropical cyclone activity, drought, and flood risk patterns globally, requiring scenario updates in catastrophe models and catastrophe bond pricing.
