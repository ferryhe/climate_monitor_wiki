## Article semantic summary

Pandemic prevention, preparedness and response (PPR) requires sustained investment to build and maintain the capacities needed to detect, prevent and respond to health emergencies. While important progress has been made in mobilizing resources for preparedness, significant financing gaps remain, particularly in low- and middle-income countries. Governments and partners are increasingly leveraging a range of financing mechanisms, including domestic resources, development financing, donor support and catalytic funding mechanisms such as the Pandemic Fund, to strengthen preparedness capacities.
