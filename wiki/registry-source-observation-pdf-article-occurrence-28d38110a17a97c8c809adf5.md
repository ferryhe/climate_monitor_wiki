New roadmap signals Africa's pathway for sustainable and resilient agriculture
IN WINDOW 17 SEP 2026 REPORT ADAPTATION FINANCE
Marking ten years of the Initiative for the Adaptation of African Agriculture, FAO released The State of African
Agricultural Adaptation, 2016–2026: Assessment and Roadmap to 2036. Forty-six of 53 African countries have
costed combined adaptation needs of USD 566 billion across national plans — about USD 56 billion a year — of
which at least USD 80 billion is specifically agricultural, and that is a floor because 22 countries have not
itemised agriculture separately. This is the most usable denominator published this fortnight for African
adaptation protection-gap and sovereign-risk work.
