60% expect property cat rates to fall, 86% expect T&Cs to loosen: Fitch reinsurance survey
IN WINDOW 23 SEP 2026 RESEARCH REINSURANCE
Fitch finds 60% expect property catastrophe pricing to decline in 2027, a fifth of those by more than 10%, and
86% expect terms and conditions to loosen (58% selectively, 28% broadly). Fewer than 10% rank property or
property cat as a capital-allocation priority. Fitch's read is that persistent excess P&C capital will push
competition into terms-and-conditions erosion rather than headline rate — the more dangerous form of softening,
because loosened attachment points and hours clauses are not visible in rate indices.
