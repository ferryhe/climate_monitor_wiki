IN WINDOW 23 SEP 2026 STATEMENT PHYSICAL RISK
Joint statement of Somalia's Federal Government and UN agencies projecting 5.4 million people in crisis-level
hunger or worse over October–December 2026, 1.5 million of them in emergency conditions, with El Niño-driven
Deyr-season flooding projected to expose 2.5–3.5 million people and displace around one million. A quantified,
country-scale El Niño impact projection of the kind directly usable for humanitarian and sovereign disaster-risk-
financing trigger design, and a live test of anticipatory-action payout timing.
