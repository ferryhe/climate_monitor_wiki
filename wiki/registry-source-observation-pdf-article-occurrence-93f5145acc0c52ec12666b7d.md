New UN Climate Change tool strengthens tracking of non-Party climate action
IN WINDOW 17 SEP 2026 DATA TRANSITION RISK
UNFCCC launched the Initiative Self-Service on the NAZCA portal, letting cooperative climate initiatives report
progress against a common framework with annual September submissions feeding the Yearbook of Global
Climate Action; more than 300 initiatives are registered, a 70% rise since 2018, with 190 actively reporting. The
relevance is transition-risk data quality: NAZCA is the principal public register of non-state commitments, and a
standardised self-reporting layer changes what evidence is available when assessing the credibility of a
counterparty's or portfolio company's pledge.
