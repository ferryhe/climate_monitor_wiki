## Article semantic summary

The Climate Risk Disclosure Survey is a risk management tool for state insurance regulators to request from insurers on an annual basis a non-confidential disclosure of the insurers; assessment and management of their climate-related risks. The current iteration of the Survey is aligned with the international Task Force on Climate-Related Financial Disclosures (TCFD). More information about reporting, past survey submissions, and analysis reports are maintained on the California Department of Insurance's website and can be accessed below.
