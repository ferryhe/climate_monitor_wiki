## Forbes: Billion Dollar Disasters — Why Insurance Is Getting More Expensive
This March 2026 Forbes article examines how climate-risk forecasters expect 2026 to accelerate the adoption of parametric insurance, particularly for small and medium-sized enterprises struggling with rising premiums from escalating natural catastrophe losses. The piece notes that parametric products — offering fast payouts triggered by predefined physical parameters (e.g., wind speed, rainfall thresholds) — are gaining traction as traditional indemnity insurance becomes unaffordable in high-risk zones. For actuaries, this signals a market shift toward index-based products and the need for new pricing frameworks tied to climate hazard metrics.
