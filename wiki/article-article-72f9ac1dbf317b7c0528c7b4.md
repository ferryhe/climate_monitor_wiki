## Report observation: climate-monitor-2026-04-16.md

The RE3ACHS project installed 271 kWp of hybrid solar photovoltaic capacity across four health facilities in Lanao del Sur to strengthen reliable healthcare, energy security and climate resilience. The initiative also trained health staff and solar installers, supported local energy planning and aims to reduce diesel dependence and greenhouse gas emissions.
