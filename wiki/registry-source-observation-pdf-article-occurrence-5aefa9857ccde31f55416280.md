IN WINDOW 17 SEP 2026 PRESS RELEASE CLIMATE AND HEALTH
At the Global Cooling Pledge Assembly in Singapore UNEP launched a Global Roadmap on Heat Resilience
covering schools, hospitals and energy systems, a Manufacturing Platform for efficient low-GWP equipment, and
the inaugural Cool Leaders awards; Brazil, Cambodia and Côte d'Ivoire launched a Nature for Cooling Challenge
backed by a USD 3 million GEF grant plus USD 33 million co-financing, targeting nature-based cooling that can
cut peak summer temperatures by up to 5°C. Global air-conditioner demand is projected to reach 5.6 billion units
by 2050, nearly triple today's level. Direct read-across to heat mortality and morbidity assumptions in life and
health portfolios, to heat-driven business interruption, and to the electricity-demand and HFC transition
exposures in the cooling value chain.
