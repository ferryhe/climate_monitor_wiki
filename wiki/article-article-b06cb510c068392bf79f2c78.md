## Report observation: climate-monitor-2026-08-03.md

Swiss Re estimates that insured losses from natural catastrophes will reach about US$148 billion in 2026, based on the long-term average, and could rise to US$320 billion in a severe scenario. It says 2025's lower losses reflected favourable variability rather than an easing of underlying risk.
