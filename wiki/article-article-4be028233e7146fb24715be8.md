## Registry article-version summary

A peer-reviewed study published in Science Advances found that marine heat waves are causing hurricanes and tropical cyclones to intensify more rapidly, resulting in 60% more billion-dollar-plus weather disasters since 1981. Marine heat waves affect more than half of landfalling tropical cyclones and are occurring closer to coastlines with increasing frequency. For insurance actuaries, this reinforces the urgency of updating catastrophe models to account for rapid intensification risk.
