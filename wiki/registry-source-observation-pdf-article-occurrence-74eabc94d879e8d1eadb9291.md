IN WINDOW 22 SEP 2026 REPORT TRANSITION RISK
Third in an FAO series on the livestock sector's contribution to food security and sustainable agrifood systems.
Livestock supplies a third of global protein and roughly 40% of agricultural GDP (about USD 1.8 trillion) while
contributing 12% of anthropogenic greenhouse gas emissions, cattle the largest share and enteric methane a
major source; emission profiles differ by system, with low-external-input systems emitting less per unit area and
high-input systems less per kilogram of output. Relevant to transition risk in agricultural lending and insurance
books, and a counterweight to blanket sectoral exclusion approaches.
