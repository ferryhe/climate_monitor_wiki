## Report observation: climate-monitor-2026-09-03.md

WRI's analysis finds that one-quarter of global crop production is exposed to highly stressed or unreliable water supplies. It distinguishes competition for irrigation water from rainfall variability affecting rainfed crops, and describes how climate change can intensify both. Recommendations include assessing local water risks, reducing food waste and improving water and land management.
