Property reinsurance prices most likely to fall 7.5% to 15% in 2027: Moody's survey
IN WINDOW 16 SEP 2026 RESEARCH REINSURANCE
Moody's survey of reinsurance buyers points to property reinsurance prices falling 7.5–15% in 2027 following
double-digit reductions in 2026, with 86% expecting declines, up from 74% who expected declines for 2026 —
buyers having under-called the severity of the 2026 softening. A meaningful minority expect falls beyond 15%,
and 28% plan to increase their use of alternative capital in 2027. Relevant to anyone setting 2027 reinsurance
cost assumptions or modelling the cost of buying additional climate-driven catastrophe capacity.
