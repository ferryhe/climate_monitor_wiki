iClimateAction publishes three new reports on the climate observation data chain
IN WINDOW 21 SEP 2026 REPORT MODEL RISK
Three deliverables assessing the observational base underpinning climate services. In-situ networks supporting
the 55 Essential Climate Variables still depend heavily on short-term research projects, in-kind contributions or
volunteers, with material gaps across Africa, South America, Southeast Asia and the polar regions; satellite
observation covers 42 of the 55 ECVs, and a February 2026 survey of 33 climate experts found communication
with decision-makers, not data access or quality, the most-cited barrier to uptake. This is the observational-input
risk sitting underneath every catastrophe model and physical-risk scorecard — known geographic gaps and
funding fragility in the underlying networks are a source of parameter uncertainty that is rarely priced.
