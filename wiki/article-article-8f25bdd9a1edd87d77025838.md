UNEP FI's updated online course on climate change risk management for bank professionals — with a 2026 schedule including English (April 13–May 1), Spanish (October 12–30), and French (October 19–November 6) cohorts — incorporates TCFD recommendations and ISSB standards. The three-week course covers climate science, transition/physical risk frameworks, regulatory evolution, stress testing methodologies. For actuaries and risk professionals, the course provides practical grounding in climate risk assessment tools directly applicable to insurance liability and investment portfolio risk modelling.
