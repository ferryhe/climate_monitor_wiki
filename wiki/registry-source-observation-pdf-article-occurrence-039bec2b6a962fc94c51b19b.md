ITU-WMO event to review risks to radio spectrum for weather and climate services
IN WINDOW 21 SEP 2026 ANNOUNCEMENT MODEL RISK
Announcement of a joint ITU–WMO symposium on 28–30 September reviewing threats to the radio frequencies
underpinning weather forecasting, climate monitoring, water management and hazard early warning as demand
for wireless and satellite services accelerates. A non-obvious systemic dependency: degradation of protected
spectrum would directly reduce forecast skill and early-warning lead time, with knock-on effects for parametric
trigger reliability and catastrophe model input quality.
