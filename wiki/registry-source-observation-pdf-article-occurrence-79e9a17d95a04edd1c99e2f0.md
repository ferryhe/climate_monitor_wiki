How Egypt is strengthening coastal adaptation in the Nile Delta
IN WINDOW 19 AUG 2026 CASE STUDY
A US$105.2m programme — a US$31.4m Green Climate Fund grant plus US$73.8m of government co-financing — has
delivered 69 of a planned 75 km of eco-engineered sea defences, seven sea-level monitoring stations and an integrated
coastal zone management plan covering roughly 1,200 km of coast, protecting 768,000 direct and 17 million indirect
beneficiaries. A rare worked example of adaptation cost per person protected, usable in coastal-flood loss-avoidance and
adaptation-benefit modelling.

### Verified information

Stretching across the northern edge of the country, the Nile Delta is one of Egypt’s most productive and densely populated regions. Home to millions of people, it supports critical infrastructure, thriving coastal communities and some of the country’s most valuable agricultural land. Yet it is also among the areas most vulnerable to climate change.
