WRI rebranded and expanded Global Forest Watch — which monitors over 600 million hectares of forest and
has served more than 9 million users from 170-plus countries since 2014 — into Global Nature Watch, extending
near-real-time alerting beyond forests to grasslands and other vegetated ecosystems, with greenhouse gas
dynamics to follow, and a preview AI and geospatial interface called Horizon. Free, near-real-time ecosystem-
change data that can underpin TNFD LEAP location assessments and nature-related exposure screening at
portfolio level.
