Who buys US carbon credits, and what are they actually buying?
IN WINDOW 15 SEP 2026 RESEARCH CARBON MARKETS
Documents a structural mismatch between policy incentive and buyer behaviour in the US voluntary market:
despite Section 45Q paying USD 180/t for direct air capture, DAC is only 2% of the largest US buyer's portfolio,
while biomass carbon removal and storage accounted for 96% of durable CDR purchase volume in 2025
(BECCS alone 69%). California's compliance programme has issued over 267 million offset credits through
2024, 81% from forest projects. The US CDR buyer base has doubled since 2022 to 69 buyers but average
purchase size has fallen — a thinning, not deepening, market.
