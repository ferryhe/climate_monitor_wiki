CatIQ raises Montreal West Island flooding insured loss estimate to C$442m
IN WINDOW 22 SEP 2026 DATA CATASTROPHE RISK
CatIQ's third estimate for the 20–21 June 2026 Montreal West Island thunderstorm flooding rises to CAD 442
million, from CAD 411 million in August and CAD 409 million initially — a 7.5% increase since August and
continued upward development. CatIQ notes pluvial flash flooding was the story of the summer for Canadian
catastrophes, with six major events between June and August across Ontario and Quebec generating over
30,000 personal property claims. Pluvial flood development patterns of this kind bear directly on IBNR
assumptions for urban flood.
