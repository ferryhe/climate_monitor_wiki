G20 Energy Abundance Ministerial Chair's Statement
IN WINDOW 16 SEP 2026 STATEMENT TRANSITION RISK
Output of the G20 Energy Abundance Ministerial held in Houston 14–16 September, structured around
affordability, supply expansion, diversification, infrastructure and resilience, and innovation. Climate language is
close to absent: the only emissions reference is a neutral list item naming “conventional sources and zero and
low emissions technologies”, with no mention of climate change, decarbonisation targets or climate finance.
Included deliberately, because for transition-risk purposes the absence is the signal — a G20 energy
communiqué stripped of emissions language, combined with the abolition of the Sustainable Finance Working
Group, lowers the probability weight on rapid-transition scenarios and raises it on delayed-and-disorderly and
hot-house pathways. That is a direct input to scenario selection in climate stress testing.
