### Verified information

This policy brief examines the potential effects of El Niño 2026–2027 on employment, incomes and working conditions in Latin America and the Caribbean. It identifies the sectors, territories and groups of workers most exposed to droughts, extreme rainfall, flooding and heatwaves, and considers related risks to food security, occupational safety and health, and child labour. The brief emphasizes that these impacts are not predetermined and outlines short-, medium- and long-term policy responses to mitigate labour market impacts and protect exposed workers and communities.
