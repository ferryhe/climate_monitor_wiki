COP31 Global Sustainable Insurance Summit to catalyze climate resilience across the economy
IN WINDOW 17 SEP 2026 PRESS RELEASE INSURANCE
UNEP and the Insurance Association of Türkiye will co-host the summit on 13–14 November 2026 at the
Insurance House, Antalya, during COP31, under the theme “The Climate Resilience Imperative”. The named
deliverable is the global launch of the PSI white paper From Vulnerability to Insurability: building resilient
communities and economies in an era of rising physical climate risk, with an agenda spanning insurability
valuation, resilient cities, MSME protection gaps, climate–nature linkages, climate-related health risk in
insurance, data gaps and low-carbon investment. This is the most directly actuarial scheduled publication on the
forward calendar and should be diarised for November.
