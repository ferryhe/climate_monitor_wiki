## It's another triple COP year: What that means for business leaders
Heading into 2026, the executive leadership of the three UN conventions – Simon Stiell (UNFCCC), Astrid Schomaker (CBD), and Yasmine Fouad (UNCCD) – are delivering a consistent, economy-forward message. Their collective framework established at the Joint Liaison Group in Bonn reinforces that climate change, biodiversity loss and land degradation are simply different symptoms of one planetary crisis. With the 2030 target deadline acting as a hard accountability marker, the triple COP sequence must deliver concrete execution, not just declarations.

## New research helps fill longstanding gap in group insurance conversions
The CIA has released GC2020: Mortality Table for Group Conversions, the first Canadian mortality table developed specifically for individuals who convert group life insurance coverage into individual policies. The research provides actuaries with a dedicated, industry-wide basis for pricing, valuation and the assessment of conversion risk. Authored by Bob Howard, FCIA, the study addresses a longstanding challenge in Canadian actuarial practice.
