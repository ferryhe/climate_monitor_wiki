WTO Public Forum 2026 spotlights role of services in driving world trade
IN WINDOW 15 SEP 2026 PRESS RELEASE
The Public Forum ran 15–17 September in Geneva under the theme “Powering the Future”, with over 4,600
participants across 113 sessions. Climate-relevant sessions included “Powering climate action through services
trade” hosted by the COP31 Presidency, EU Deforestation Regulation enablers and green trade rules, green
corridors and African transport services, environmental services trade, circular services, critical minerals supply-
chain resilience, and Fisheries Subsidies Agreement implementation. No climate-specific WTO publication other
than the World Trade Report launched at the Forum.
