## Registry article-version summary

A World Economic Forum article published March 2026 argues that the insurance industry, having paid $100 billion in natural catastrophe insured losses in 2025, is uniquely positioned to drive climate adaptation through risk-based pricing, pre-disaster resilience incentives, and parametric product innovation. The piece highlights that 92% of 2025 natural disaster losses were weather-related, underscoring the systemic materiality of climate risk for the sector.

## Report observation: climate-monitor-2026-04-20.md

Insurance de-risks and underwrites private investment across the energy-water-climate nexus and can move beyond post-disaster payouts to finance resilience before shocks occur. The article calls for risk reduction at source, pre-disaster investment, fair risk sharing, better data paired with public decisions and equitable risk-based pricing, illustrating these principles with peatland flood protection and parametric cover for heat and flooding.
