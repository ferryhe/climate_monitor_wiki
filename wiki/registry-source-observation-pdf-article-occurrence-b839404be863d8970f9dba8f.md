Cat 5 Florida hurricanes could drive $300bn plus insurance market loss: Swiss Re
IN WINDOW 16 SEP 2026 RESEARCH CATASTROPHE RISK
Swiss Re Institute models a Cat 5 into Miami or Tampa Bay at over USD 300 billion of market loss, a repeat of
the 1926 Great Miami hurricane at more than USD 200 billion, and a repeat of Hurricane Andrew at close to USD
100 billion. The USD 200 billion spread between the Andrew repeat and a modern Miami/Tampa Cat 5 is driven
almost entirely by landfall location. Against an estimated USD 148 billion of global insured cat losses in a normal
2026, an extreme Florida event would take the annual total past USD 450 billion. Directly usable for tail-scenario
and capital-adequacy stress testing.
