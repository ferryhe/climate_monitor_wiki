Urban Flood and Gully Erosion Risk Management in the Congo Basin
IN WINDOW 17 SEP 2026 REPORT PHYSICAL RISK
Exemplar case study with the World Bank on investment programmes across the Congo River Basin, covering
urban resilience to flood and erosion hazards alongside infrastructure access, capacity building and emergency
response. Gully erosion is a material and poorly modelled loss driver in rapidly urbanising African cities, and this
is one of the few structured treatments of it in the adaptation literature.
