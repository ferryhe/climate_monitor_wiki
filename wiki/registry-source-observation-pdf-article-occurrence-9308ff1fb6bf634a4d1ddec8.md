# EFRAG joins the PCAF Engagement Group

## PDF report observation: pdf-article-occurrence-9308ff1fb6bf634a4d1ddec8

EFRAG joins the PCAF Engagement Group
IN WINDOW 12 AUG 2026 PRESS RELEASE
EFRAG, the European Commission's technical adviser on sustainability reporting, has joined PCAF's Engagement Group
for Central Banks and Financial Regulators, explicitly to strengthen the link between the ESRS and greenhouse gas
accounting methodology. Signals convergence between European mandatory disclosure and the PCAF financed-
emissions methodology used in transition-risk work, reducing the risk of divergent Scope 3 category 15 measurement
bases across jurisdictions. PCAF now reports over 750 financial institution signatories.
