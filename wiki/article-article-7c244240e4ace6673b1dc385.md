## Article semantic summary

The Global Initiative on AI for Health invites participation in the GI-AI4H Benchmarking Challenge, an open global challenge through which health AI models can be evaluated using clinical data without disclosing the data to the model developer or the model to the institution holding the data. The challenge tests privacy-preserving evaluation approaches that allow clinical data to remain under the control of the institutions that hold it while submitted models are evaluated securely. The challenge is designed for evaluation rather than model training.
