Climate-resilient T&D networks in Africa
IN WINDOW 18 SEP 2026 REPORT PHYSICAL RISK
Technical brief framing climate risk assessment for electricity transmission and distribution networks and
integrating resilience measures across the project cycle, covering extreme heat, flooding, wildfire, landslide, and
sand and dust storms against network reliability, asset life and financial viability, with evidence from the
Mauritania–Mali Interconnection, Eastern Ethiopia Grid Reinforcement and Mozambique's Songo-Matambo. The
claimed cost–benefit evidence for adaptation spend is precisely the evidence base most often missing when
pricing resilience investment.
