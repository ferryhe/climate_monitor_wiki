The four North American actuarial societies (American Academy of Actuaries, CIA, CAS, SOA) released updated data disclosures for the Actuaries Climate Index, providing revised values through fall 2023 and methodology notes. The ACI provides an objective, retrospective measure of changes in extreme weather frequency, sea level, and temperature extremes across the US and Canada, serving as a foundational dataset for climate-adjusted insurance risk pricing.
