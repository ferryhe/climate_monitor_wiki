# Supply concerns drive FAO Food Price Index higher in August

Supply concerns drive FAO Food Price Index higher in August
IN WINDOW 4 SEP 2026 PRESS RELEASE
The FAO Food Price Index averaged 133.3 points in August 2026, up 1.9% from July and 2.5% year-on-year.
FAO linked supply concerns to high temperatures and other weather risks, alongside geopolitical and trade disruptions.
Hot, dry weather weakened European wheat prospects and milk supply. El Niño-related conditions affected palm-oil
supply expectations and the global rice forecast. FAO's lower global maize forecast reflects worsening prospects
in France and Poland. These observations connect physical climate risks with measured food commodity prices.
