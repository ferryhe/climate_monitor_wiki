## Registry article-version summary

EIOPA raises the alarm about Europe's significant protection gap against natural disasters: only ~25% of nat-cat losses in the EU have been insured over the past decades. Historical data is no longer a reliable predictor of future losses as climate change increases frequency and severity of floods, droughts, storms, and wildfires. Calls for systemic solutions including public-private mechanisms.
