## Report observation: climate-monitor-2026-09-03.md

Sustax combines ERA5 historical data and bias-corrected CMIP6 projections to assess climate conditions favourable to processionary caterpillars in a Spanish municipality. Temperature and precipitation thresholds are compared across three emissions scenarios through 2080. The results describe climatic suitability for outbreaks, rather than predicting the outbreaks themselves, and support local health and land-use planning.
