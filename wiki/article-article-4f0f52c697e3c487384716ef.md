## Article semantic summary

Drawing on research conducted between 2023 and 2025 in collaboration with IPPF in Colombia, Ghana, Morocco and Togo, the study provides one of the most comprehensive assessments to date of how women and men perceive existing and emerging male contraceptive methods. Participants will explore findings on knowledge, attitudes and contraceptive behaviors, and discuss how new reversible male contraceptives could expand choice, promote shared responsibility for family planning, and help inform the future positioning of male methods within the contraceptive method mix.
