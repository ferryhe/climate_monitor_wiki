Strengthening Hurricane Polo brings Mexico catastrophe bond into focus
IN WINDOW 23 SEP 2026 DEAL PARAMETRIC
Hurricane Polo's intensification put the USD 175 million IBRD CAR Mexico 2024 (Pacific) parametric cat bond in
focus. The bond triggers on central pressure and location, requiring central pressure at or below 937 mb, with
payouts of 25% to 100% of principal by intensity and landfall location. Polo reached Category 5 with central
pressure as low as 892 mb while offshore, then weakened to 120 mph and 954 mb approaching Baja landfall,
making a trigger unlikely. A textbook illustration of parametric basis risk: peak intensity far inside the trigger
threshold, but in the wrong place at the wrong time.
