## Registry article-version summary

The 2026 UN Financing for Sustainable Development Report finds that development progress is imperiled by global fragmentation and a financing squeeze facing developing countries, with official development assistance falling 7.1% in 2024 and a further 23.1% in 2025 — the steepest annual drop on record. Developing nations face a $4.3 trillion annual financing gap for the SDGs. For climate actuaries, this signals mounting fiscal pressure on sovereigns to absorb climate losses and potential knock-on effects for insurance and reinsurance market capacity.
