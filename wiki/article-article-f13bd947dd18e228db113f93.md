## Registry article-version summary

The World Bank approved a $35 million financing package (March 20, 2026) for Samoa's Pacific Region Preparedness, Adaptation and Resilience (PREPARE) project, targeting 180,000 Samoans (80% of the population) across multi-hazard early warning systems, resilient infrastructure upgrades, and risk-informed urban planning. The project includes $500,000 in pre-arranged emergency response finance and will train 300+ personnel in multi-hazard early warning — a model for rapid disaster risk finance deployment increasingly watched by insurers and reinsurers active in the Pacific.
