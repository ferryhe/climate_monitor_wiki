Best Bets for Gender-Responsive Digital Climate Advisory Services (DCAS)
IN WINDOW 18 SEP 2026 REPORT INDEX INSURANCE
Technical brief on climate advisory services delivered by SMS, app, USSD, voice, radio and helpline, arguing
that services designed on an assumption of equal farmer access amplify rather than reduce disparities, and that
advisories must be linked to the inputs farmers need to act on them. Directly relevant to index and parametric
agricultural insurance: advisory reach and trust determine whether climate information changes behaviour, which
bears on both basis risk and take-up assumptions in smallholder index products.
Caveat. No quantified usage or outcome statistics on the landing page.
