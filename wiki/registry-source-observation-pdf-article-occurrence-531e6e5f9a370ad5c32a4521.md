CCRIF lifts parametric risk pool coverage another 14.5% to $1.8bn for current policy year
IN WINDOW 15 SEP 2026 DEAL PARAMETRIC
CCRIF SPC's parametric risk pool has grown to USD 1.8 billion of coverage limits for the current policy year, up
14.5% on June 2026 and 25% on the prior year. Membership stands at 39, with four new members this season
— Jamaica Public Service Company, National Water Commission Jamaica, Nevis Electricity Company and
Nevis Water Department — all utilities, evidencing extension beyond sovereigns into water and electric utilities.
Since 2007 CCRIF has made 82 payouts totalling USD 483 million, disbursed within 14 days of a qualifying
event. The clearest sovereign-risk-pool datapoint of the window and a working example of parametric basis-risk
design at scale.
