Electrification
IN WINDOW 22 SEP 2026 REPORT TRANSITION RISK
IEA flagship. Electricity demand grew over 3% a year in 2015–2025, twice the pace of overall energy demand;
EVs reached nearly 25% of new car sales in 2025 from under 1% in 2015; electricity is about 23% of total final
energy consumption. Under the High Electrification Scenario the global electrification rate reaches 35% by 2035,
with transport moving from 2% to 13%; HES paired with accelerated low-emissions generation cuts end-use
emissions 40% by 2035 and total energy-sector emissions by more than half from 2025 levels. Included on the
transition-risk test rather than as market commentary: it supplies scenario inputs (StePS versus HES) and
emissions trajectories usable directly in transition scenario analysis.
