Asking Better Questions on Nature: For Bank Chief Risk Officers and Risk Committees
IN WINDOW SEPTEMBER 2026 GUIDANCE NATURE RISK
A TNFD guide produced with UNEP FI, structured around ten questions for bank chief risk officers and risk
committees to test exposure to nature-related financial risk across credit and investment portfolios, with the
headline quantification that pollinator decline alone puts up to USD 577 billion of annual global crop production at
risk. It positions nature loss as a systemic financial-stability issue to be handled inside mainstream risk
frameworks rather than as a disclosure exercise, and is the bank-CRO companion to TNFD's earlier CFO and
asset-owner editions. Relevant to actuaries working on nature-related financial risk and on agricultural credit and
crop exposures.
