## Article semantic summary

The Casualty Actuarial Society (CAS), through its Climate and Sustainability Working Group, is soliciting research proposals and awarding up to two projects that support the development of innovative actuarial approaches to understanding, quantifying, and managing the climate risks facing insurers. While awards in the $40,000-$50,000 range are typical, proposals may request up to $75,000 with clear justification (e.g., data acquisition, model licensing, and/or multi-peril or multi-geography scope). Proposals should be submitted by May 22, 2026.

## Registry article-version summary

The Casualty Actuarial Society (CAS) has issued a Request for Proposals (RFP) awarding up to $75,000 for research that develops innovative actuarial approaches to climate risk, covering five tracks: perils and hazard climate change, actuarial practice integration, mitigation and adaptation investment, insurability and market structure, and risk transfer and climate-related capital. Proposals due May 22, 2026; completed projects due February 1, 2027. The RFP explicitly targets practical tools and case studies across primary insurers, reinsurers, and capital markets.
