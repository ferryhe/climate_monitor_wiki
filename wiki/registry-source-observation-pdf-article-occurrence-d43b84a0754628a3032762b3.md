As cat bond loss multiples tighten, AM Best urges investors towards greater selectivity
IN WINDOW 25 SEP 2026 RESEARCH CAT BONDS
AM Best reports cat bond loss multiples compressing to 2.5x in H1 2026 from 3.28x in H1 2025, with 2026 year-
to-date at 2.4x against 3.0x in 2025 and 3.71x in 2024. Weighted average spread fell 96 basis points while
expected loss on new issuance rose 30 basis points against H1 2025 — investors are being paid less for more
risk. Returns at 30 June 2026: Swiss Re Global Cat Bond Index 4.1%, ILS Advisers Index 3.9%. The
simultaneous fall in multiple and rise in expected loss is the sharpest risk-adjusted-return warning of the window
and bears directly on the cost and reliability of alternative capacity into the January 2027 renewals.
