Zambezi Basin Countries Agree on Climate Resilience Priorities for $130 Million Program
IN WINDOW 23 SEP 2026 PRESS RELEASE
Eight riparian states — Angola, Botswana, Malawi, Mozambique, Namibia, Tanzania, Zambia and Zimbabwe —
agreed priorities for a USD 130 million regional resilience programme with the Zambezi Watercourse
Commission, structured around natural resources management, community climate resilience, institutional
capacity and coordination, and recommending members allocate at least 10% of national budgets to programme
priorities. The basin supports over 380 million people dependent on hydropower, agriculture, fisheries and
drinking water, making it one of the most concentrated transboundary drought and hydrological risks on the
continent and a natural candidate for regional index-based risk transfer.
