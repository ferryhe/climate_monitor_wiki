## Verified article content

For insurers, climate-related risks and opportunities are key topics affecting the industry’s core business. The scientific consensus is that a continued rise in average global temperatures will have a significant effect on weather-related natural catastrophes and will account for an increasingly large share of natural catastrophe losses. For example, property and casualty companies that write business relating to catastrophes will have a heightened focus on the impacts of climate-related risks.
