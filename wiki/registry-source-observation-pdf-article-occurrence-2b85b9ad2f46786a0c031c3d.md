Fostering micro-disaster risk finance in Emerging Asia
IN WINDOW 24 SEP 2026 REPORT DISASTER RISK FINANCING
OECD Policy Brief on closing the household and micro-enterprise protection gap in Emerging Asia, a region that
suffered USD 685 billion of disaster damage over 2010–2024, the large majority uninsured. It assesses
microinsurance and micro-catastrophe bonds as delivery mechanisms and concludes that community-level
exposure data and local risk knowledge are the binding constraint on product design — which is to say, on
pricing and basis risk in index-based micro-covers for a segment that is very thinly modelled.
