## Article semantic summary

UNDP and the International Airport Administrative Institute (IAI) convened a high-level roundtable in Rome on 25 March 2026 to advance climate and development finance partnerships, building on the UNDP Rome Centre's PISTA platform designed to help partner countries move from climate plans to financeable projects. The roundtable focused on attracting private capital for climate action and enhancing the operational capacity of national aviation and infrastructure stakeholders to access climate finance — relevant to insurers interested in infrastructure risk and public-private partnership models.
