Rescinding US SEC's Disclosure Rule Will Blindfold Investors to Climate Risk
UPDATED 4 AUG PUBLISHED 31 JUL 2026 COMMENTARY
Argues against rescission of the SEC's 2024 climate disclosure rule, citing 320 institutional investors managing US$50tn
backing standardised disclosure, an average of 20 separate billion-dollar US weather disasters annually, and US$2.3tn of
global energy-transition investment in 2025. Arguably the most consequential item this cycle for practitioners: loss of
mandatory US physical- and transition-risk disclosure degrades the input data available for portfolio climate risk
assessment and pension-fund scenario work.
