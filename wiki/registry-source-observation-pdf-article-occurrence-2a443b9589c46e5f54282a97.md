with adaptation's share of tagged spend falling from 81.9% in 2022 as mitigation rose to 30.8% by 2024; it estimates
US$62.3bn of investment is needed, 78–86% of it private. Relevant to assessing transition-finance gaps and the

### Verified information

Around the world, climate change impacts are increasingly straining both public and private finance. With extreme weather events becoming more frequent and more severe, temperatures reaching new record highs and precipitation patterns being significantly altered, we are experiencing profound impacts on our economies, ecosystems and the livelihoods and well-being of our communities. For example, Chile has been experiencing a mega-drought for more than 15 years, which has caused heavy economic damage by negatively impacting water-reliant sectors like agriculture, mining and energy production.
