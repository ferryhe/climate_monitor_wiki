BMA to prioritise substance of parametric risk transfer over contractual form for new PSPI
IN WINDOW 17 SEP 2026 GUIDANCE PARAMETRIC
The Bermuda Monetary Authority published a stakeholder letter responding to feedback on its January 2026
consultation for a dedicated Parametric Special Purpose Insurer class supporting structured, fully collateralised
parametric risk transfer. The BMA will look to the substance of risk transfer rather than contractual form,
admitting contracts structured as reinsurance, derivatives or swaps provided statutory requirements are met;
existing SPIs may transition, and some may write both indemnity and parametric business case by case. An
amendment to the Insurance Act 1978 is being drafted for Q4 2026. The most consequential regulatory
development of the window for parametric and index-based climate covers.
