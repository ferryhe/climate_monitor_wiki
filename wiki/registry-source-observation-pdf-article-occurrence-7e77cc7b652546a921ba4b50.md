### Verified information

As part of the global Flash Flood Guidance System (FFGS) project, the meetings marked an important milestone, serving as the final Steering Committee meetings under the current project grant from the US Department of State. They brought together senior representatives from the World Meteorological Organization (WMO), participating countries and regional partners to review progress, share experiences, and discuss priorities for sustaining the work beyond the project period. The high-level participation reflected the importance of continued regional cooperation on flash flood early warning.
