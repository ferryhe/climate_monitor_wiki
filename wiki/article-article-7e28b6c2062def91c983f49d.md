## Article semantic summary

This checklist is intended to provide a summary of required climate-related disclosures set out in the IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards (ISSB Standards) and related guidance. Entities should refer directly to the relevant ISSB Standards for the comprehensive disclosure requirements and additional context. An entity that prepares sustainability-related financial disclosures in accordance with the ISSB Standards must always apply IFRS S1 General Requirements for Disclosure of Sustainability-related Financial Information (IFRS S1).
