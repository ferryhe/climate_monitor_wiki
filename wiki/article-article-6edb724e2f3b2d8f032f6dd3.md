LONDON (September 3, 2026) — The UK Government today announced it will make a £400 million investment in the Tropical Forest Forever Facility via a loan, a mechanism launched last year to provide long-term, predictable financing to countries that protect and sustainably manage their tropical forests. The UK has partnered with Brazil in developing the TFFF since its inception. This announcement follows previous pledges from Brazil, Indonesia, France, Germany, Norway and several philanthropic institutions.
