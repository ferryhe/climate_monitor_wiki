4 lessons from young people on the frontlines of the Nepal-Tibet floods
IN WINDOW 24 SEP 2026 BLOG CATASTROPHE RISK
Post-event analysis of the 26 August 2026 flood triggered when an ice-and-rock avalanche dammed a tributary
of the Bhotekoshi River on the Nepal–China border, with 1,300-plus confirmed dead and 5,000-plus missing
across Rasuwa, Nuwakot and Dhading. The central point for catastrophe modellers is that the hazard originated
in Tibet, outside Nepal's monitoring network, so no national early-warning system saw it coming — a
transboundary GLOF and landslide-dam blind spot that will recur across the Himalayas as glacial melt
accelerates.
