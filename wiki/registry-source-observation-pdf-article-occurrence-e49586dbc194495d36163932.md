carbon projects, built from over 17,000 price inputs a year and refreshed three times weekly; it closed at USD
6.30/t on 18 September 2026 against a 2026 range of USD 3.16–7.07 and a 2026 average to date of USD 6.04
(2024: USD 3.79). The companion Tradable20 index has averaged USD 2.58 below the AO500 in 2026. For
actuaries the AO500/Tradable20 spread is a direct, published measure of liquidity-tier basis risk in carbon-credit
valuation.
