Busy August pushes Allstate's current aggregate year pre-tax cat losses to $3.15bn
IN WINDOW 17 SEP 2026 DATA REINSURANCE
Allstate reported USD 748 million of pre-tax catastrophe losses in August 2026 from 21 separate events, half
from a single wind and hail occurrence, taking April–August aggregate pre-tax cat losses to USD 3.15 billion. Its
only current annual aggregate protection is the USD 150 million Class B tranche of Sanders Re III (Series 2023-
1), attaching at USD 4.78 billion with a USD 50 million per-event deductible, while a new USD 1 billion aggregate
excess-of-loss layer for 2026 attaches at USD 8.5 billion with a USD 1 million event deductible. A clean worked
illustration of how per-event deductibles inside aggregate structures create qualifying-loss uncertainty — a live
reserving and disclosure problem.
