IN WINDOW 14 SEP 2026 BLOG NATURE RISK

USD 220 billion to nature-based solutions, a 30:1 ratio, with USD 22 billion a year in harmful fishing subsidies
prior to the 2025 WTO agreement. Argues capital alone is insufficient without redesigned incentives and
institutions. A useful counterweight to the TNFD adopter numbers when assessing whether disclosure is actually
shifting capital.
