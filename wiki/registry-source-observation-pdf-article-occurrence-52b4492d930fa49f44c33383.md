Navigating the Steel Transition amid Global Excess Capacity
IN WINDOW 22 SEP 2026 REPORT TRANSITION RISK
OECD Science, Technology and Industry Policy Paper on how steel overcapacity blocks decarbonisation:
excess capacity depresses prices, squeezes margins, increases earnings volatility and borrowing costs, and
limits internal financing for technology deployment, while raising the relative price premium of low-emission steel
and weakening the demand signal. It warns that badly designed low-emission subsidies may themselves trigger
new waves of excess capacity. Opened and confirmed as a transition-risk paper rather than a trade paper;
directly relevant to credit and equity exposure to hard-to-abate industrials.
