## Registry article-version summary

A UNCTAD statistical report (published April 7, 2026 via China's Economic Daily) finds that most current climate finance is not genuinely additional — much of it represents reclassified existing official development assistance, with only a handful of donor countries meeting the 0.7% GNI ODA baseline. The report identifies widespread double-counting of projects simultaneously classified as ODA and climate finance, abuse of climate-marker systems for non-climate projects, and a growing share of non-concessional (market-rate) loans that risk worsening recipient country debt burdens.
