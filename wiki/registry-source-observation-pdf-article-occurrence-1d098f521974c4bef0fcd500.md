# African Development Bank to back green hydrogen projects in Egypt, Morocco, Namibia and South Africa with grants worth a total of $20 million

African Development Bank to back green hydrogen projects in Egypt, Morocco, Namibia and South
Africa with grants worth a total of $20 million
IN WINDOW 18 SEP 2026 PRESS RELEASE TRANSITION RISK
USD 20 million of reimbursable SEFA grants (board approval pending) across four projects — Project Ra in
Egypt (USD 3.55m, sustainable aviation fuels), Guelmim Green Hydrogen Valley in Morocco (USD 5.28m),
Hyphen in Namibia (USD 5.93m, marine fuels) and Saldanha Hydrogen DRI in South Africa (USD 5.24m, low-
carbon iron) — selected from 81 applications across 18 countries. Together they represent roughly USD 23
billion of total investment, 20 GW of renewable capacity, 7 GW of electrolysers and 2,950 MWh of storage: a
concrete read on the scale and construction-phase risk profile of African hydrogen build-out.
