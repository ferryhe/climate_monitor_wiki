The Partnership for Carbon Accounting Financials (PCAF) Strengthens Global Board
Representation by Appointing Three New Directors
IN WINDOW 21 SEP 2026 PRESS RELEASE GOVERNANCE
PCAF appointed Neil Atkinson (Riyad Bank), José Luis Muñoz (Grupo Financiero Banorte) and Dafina
Nonkulovska (Rabobank) to its board, explicitly to widen emerging-market representation in the governance of
the Global GHG Accounting and Reporting Standard. Borderline for this report as governance rather than
methodology, but included because board composition steers the 2026–2028 standard agenda, two of whose
seven priority topics are new insurance methodologies — insurance claims, and personal non-life insurance —
which will determine how non-life insurers are expected to account for underwriting emissions.
