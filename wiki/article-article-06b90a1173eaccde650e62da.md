Canada's major property and casualty insurers — including Intact Financial, TD Insurance, Wawanesa, and Definity — are urging homeowners to flood- and fire-proof their properties as climate-related claims surge, with home-insurance premiums rising approximately 6% last year. CEOs are publicly calling on Prime Minister Mark Carney to reprioritise climate risk in government policy, warning that the current economic focus on the oil sector is crowding out adaptation measures. For actuaries, this signals intensifying physical risk pricing pressure and potential systemic capacity constraints in Canadian wildfire and flood zones.
