## Article semantic summary

The Adaptation Fund has approved a $30 million programme to scale locally led climate action across Eswatini, Zambia, and Zimbabwe, focusing on water security, livelihoods, and ecosystem resilience. The initiative reflects a growing trend toward devolving climate adaptation finance directly to local communities — a development that has material implications for the design of adaptation-linked insurance products and resilience bond structures in sub-Saharan Africa.
