ILS can play a broader role in narrowing the global protection gap: Moody's
IN WINDOW 24 SEP 2026 RESEARCH PROTECTION GAP
Moody's argues P&C insurers and reinsurers cannot close the protection gap alone given capital and regulatory
constraints, and positions insurance-linked securities as the marginal capacity source. Alternative capital
reached a record USD 147 billion in H1 2026; H1 2026 cat bond issuance set a record of nearly USD 18 billion;
outstanding cat bond market size was USD 65.6 billion at end-Q2 2026. Moody's cites Hurricane Melissa
(October 2025), which caused an estimated USD 12.2 billion of damage in Jamaica — 54% of GDP — and
triggered a full payout on a World Bank-backed USD 150 million parametric cat bond.
