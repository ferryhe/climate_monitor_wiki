## ISO 14091:2021 — Vulnerability Assessment Framework for Climate-Charged Hazards
ISO 14091:2021 provides a structured framework for assessing climate change vulnerability of assets, systems, and networks. Supports physical risk assessment aligned with TCFD and ISSB frameworks. Applicable to actuarial modelling of climate-exposed insurance portfolios.
