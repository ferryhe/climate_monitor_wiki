IN WINDOW 21 SEP 2026 EVENT ADAPTATION FINANCE
The SCF's annual Forum ran 21–22 September in Sydney, hosted by Australia with Pacific Island countries and
the Philippines, followed by the 41st SCF meeting 23–25 September, covering barriers to financing, enabling
environments, financial instruments and delivery mechanisms, and drawing on submissions from 31
organisations and country groups. Water-sector adaptation finance is the thinnest-covered segment of the
protection-gap picture and SCF Forum output typically feeds the COP finance agenda.
Caveat. Held in-window; no Forum summary report had been published as at 28 September 2026. Webcasts of both
Forum days are posted.
