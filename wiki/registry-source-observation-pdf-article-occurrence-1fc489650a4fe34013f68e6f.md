Building Colombia's Nature Economy Could Create Hundreds of Thousands of New Jobs
IN WINDOW 15 SEP 2026 RESEARCH TRANSITION RISK
Estimates 775,000 jobs from nature tourism and agribusiness in Colombia by 2035 against a government-
identified pipeline of more than USD 32 billion in green investment opportunities. The transition-risk figure of note
is that about 4.7 million workers, roughly 22.6% of the current workforce, will be directly affected by the climate
transition, in a labour market where around 55% of workers were informally employed as of June 2026. A
concrete just-transition exposure quantification for an emerging-market sovereign, relevant both to sovereign
transition-risk scenarios and to life and health portfolios exposed to informal-sector displacement.
