IN WINDOW 21 SEP 2026 STATEMENT TRANSITION RISK
Simon Stiell's keynote to the Build Clean Now Industry Acceleration Summit in New York puts 2025 renewable
additions at 693 GW and avoided fossil-fuel cost at close to USD 500 billion, against clean-energy investment
now above USD 2 trillion a year, with 19 commercial-scale clean projects worth roughly USD 43 billion reaching
final investment decision in H1 2026. He restates the shift in the central warming projection from about 4°C to
2.6°C, the Baku-to-Belém USD 1.3 trillion mobilisation goal, and a target of electricity at 35% of global final
energy by 2035. The 2.6°C figure and the USD 1.3 trillion target are the reference points regulators and scenario
providers will anchor to.
