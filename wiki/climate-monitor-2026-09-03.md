## Monitoring Surface Water with SAR for Water Resource Management
Monitoring surface water is critical for supporting agriculture, sustaining ecosystem health, and managing water resources. The recent availability of Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) data from the NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar (NISAR) mission provides users with high-resolution, consistent observations of Earth's surface every 12 days. These observations are particularly valuable in tropical and flood-prone regions, where frequent cloud cover and varying illumination often limit the effectiveness of optical remote sensing.

## Low Water Levels Lake Powell Lake Mead August 2026
The two largest reservoirs in the United States approached record low levels in the summer of 2026. Short- and long-term droughts, plus below-average winter snowfalls, diminished water supplies across the Colorado River watershed, which feeds into Lake Powell and Lake Mead.
