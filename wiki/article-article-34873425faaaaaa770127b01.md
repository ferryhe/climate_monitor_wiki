## Article semantic summary

The European Insurance and Occupational Pensions Authority (EIOPA) has emerged as a significant voice in the ongoing EU debate over establishing a natural catastrophe (natcat) risk pool. The discussion addresses how a pan-European pool could help insurers manage escalating climate-related catastrophe losses and narrow the protection gap. For actuaries, EIOPA's position signals potential shifts in regulatory capital requirements and risk-sharing mechanisms that could affect pricing and reserving practices across the EU insurance market.
