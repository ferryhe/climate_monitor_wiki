## Article semantic summary

The Trustees of the IFRS Foundation announced the formation of the International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB) on 3 November 2021 at COP26 in Glasgow, following strong market demand for its establishment. The ISSB is developing—in the public interest—standards that will result in a high-quality, comprehensive global baseline of sustainability disclosures focused on the needs of investors and the financial markets. Sustainability factors are becoming a mainstream part of investment decision-making.

## Registry article-version summary

ISSB officially renamed its BEES project to "Nature-related Disclosures," expanding scope beyond biodiversity. The project will supplement IFRS S1 and S2, aligned with TNFD, SASB, GRI, and ESRS. Consultation draft planned ahead of COP17 (October 2026).
