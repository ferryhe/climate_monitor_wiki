Nepal floods damage 1,800 hectares of crops and kill thousands of livestock
IN WINDOW 10 SEP 2026 PRESS RELEASE
Details the 26 August Bhote Koshi–Trishuli flood: over 1,000 deaths, roughly 1,800 hectares of mostly paddy
destroyed, nearly 7,700 tonnes of production lost, 5–10% of livestock and poultry lost across 11 municipalities,
and 41 bridges destroyed. A rare event-level dataset of agricultural and infrastructure loss quantities suitable for
calibrating flood catastrophe and index-insurance parameters, with an explicit second-order missed-planting-
season loss channel.
