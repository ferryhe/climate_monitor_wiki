## Registry article-version summary

Joint EIOPA-ESM proposal for a European natural catastrophe pool to reduce Europe's protection gap (~75% of economic losses uninsured) and strengthen resilience against increasingly frequent and severe disasters. Primary insurers and reinsurers alone may not have sufficient capacity. The proposed mechanism would complement national schemes and market-based solutions.
