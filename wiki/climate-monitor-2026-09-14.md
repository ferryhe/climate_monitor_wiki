## As the US Carbon Dioxide Removal Market Retreats, Other Governments Step Up
The article explains that Paris-consistent modeled pathways require carbon dioxide removal of 2.7–4.1 billion metric tons annually by 2035 and 6.5–13.3 billion by 2050, alongside steep emissions reductions, to support net zero and eventual temperature decline after overshoot. It also examines government policies, certification frameworks, monitoring and reporting protocols, and safeguards intended to scale carbon removal while addressing permanence and environmental risks.

## Statement: UNEP Report Warns World is Soon to Exceed 1.5°C ‒ but Return is Possible
UNEP finds global warming is likely to cross 1.5°C within the next few years, increasing risks from extreme heat, floods, fires and droughts, while temperatures could later return toward Paris Agreement goals through deep emissions cuts and responsible carbon removal. The statement emphasizes urgent adaptation and resilience to address unavoidable impacts and reduce the chance that shocks become catastrophes.
