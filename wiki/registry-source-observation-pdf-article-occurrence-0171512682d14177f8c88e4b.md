COP31 President-Designate addresses WMO
IN WINDOW 16 SEP 2026 PRESS RELEASE
Turkish Environment Minister Murat Kurum set out the COP31 presidency's ten priority themes at WMO
headquarters, including climate-resilient cities, resilient health systems, food security, oceans, and a “Climate
Implementation Bridge”, and stated that strengthening observation, forecasting and early warning capacity is part
of the COP31 implementation approach. The presidency's emphasis on measurable implementation over
pledges, and on resilient cities and health systems, indicates where insurance-relevant COP31 outcomes are
most likely to land.
